# 01 — Product Overview

## Product Name

SMEFlow AI

## Problem

Many small businesses still manage customers, suppliers, stock, quotations, orders and invoices using separate spreadsheets, messaging applications and manual records.

This creates problems such as:

- Stock information becoming inaccurate
- Duplicate or inconsistent customer records
- Difficulty tracking quotations and orders
- Poor visibility into business performance
- Repetitive manual reporting
- Business data existing without useful insights

## Proposed Solution

SMEFlow AI is a lightweight business management platform that centralizes key SME operations into a single system.

The system will allow users to manage operational records and later use an AI assistant to ask business questions using their own structured business data.

Examples:

- Which products are running low?
- Which products generated the most revenue this month?
- Which customers purchased the most?
- What should I reorder soon?
- Show me outstanding invoices.
- Summarize this month's sales performance.

## Target User

Primary target user:

- Owner or staff member of a small-to-medium trading business

The design is inspired by realistic operations such as hardware, electrical, wholesale and retail businesses.

## Project Objectives

1. Build a production-style full-stack web application.
2. Model realistic SME business workflows.
3. Demonstrate relational database design and backend business logic.
4. Implement secure APIs and authentication.
5. Provide useful dashboards and reporting.
6. Integrate AI into real structured business workflows.
7. Deploy the complete system publicly for portfolio demonstration.

## MVP Scope

### Included

- User authentication
- Company profile
- Product management
- Product categories
- Inventory tracking
- Customers
- Suppliers
- Quotations
- Sales orders
- Purchase orders
- Invoices
- Dashboard
- AI business assistant

### Not Included Initially

- Full accounting / general ledger
- Payroll
- Tax filing
- Multi-warehouse inventory
- Point-of-sale hardware integration
- Real payment gateway
- Advanced ERP manufacturing workflows

These can be considered future enhancements.

## Success Criteria

The project is successful when a demo user can:

1. Sign in.
2. Create products, customers and suppliers.
3. Create a purchase order.
4. Receive stock and see inventory increase.
5. Create a quotation for a customer.
6. Convert a quotation into a sales order.
7. Confirm a sale and see stock decrease.
8. Generate an invoice.
9. View business KPIs.
10. Ask the AI assistant questions about the business data.
